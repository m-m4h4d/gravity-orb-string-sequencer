✦ Orbfall · the gravity orb & string sequencer

Drop orbs. Bend gravity. Hear it play.

Orbfall turns falling orbs into music. They strike six tuned guitar strings and three drum lanes, bounce off angled deflectors you place, and every hit is written onto a live sheet-music staff that you can replay or export as MIDI.

Features

Everything it does

Part pinball machine, part step sequencer, part notation app. All of it runs in a single page.

Six retunable guitar strings

Each string is a lane tuned to a note of the active scale. Orbs pluck them as they cross, and the string visibly vibrates from the point of impact.

  • 6 strings
  • stereo panned by position

Three drum lanes

Below the strings sit a synthesized hi-hat, snare and kick. Rhythm comes from the same physics as the melody.

  • Hi-Hat
  • Snare
  • Kick

2D gravity orbs

Orbs fall with real velocity, leave glowing trails and ricochet up to 18 times before fading. Up to 14 can be in play at once.

  • gravity 180–680
  • auto-drop emitter

Angled geometric deflectors

Place bars, triangles and diamonds on any lane to build pinball-style cascades. Click one to rotate it 30°, and right-click or double-click to remove it.

  • bar
  • triangle
  • diamond

Three musical scales

Switch the strings between scales on the fly. Every lane retunes immediately and plays an upward preview run of the new scale.

  • Pentatonic
  • Dorian
  • Major

Drag to strum

Sweep your cursor or finger across the strings to strum them by hand and play along with the orbs. Works with mouse, pen and touch.

✦ Space FX

One toggle sends plucks and percussion through a stereo ping-pong delay and a 2.8-second procedurally generated reverb, so the music gets a wide ambient wash.

  • ping-pong delay
  • convolver reverb

Live sheet music transcription

Every hit is written onto a scrolling five-line staff as it happens: guitar notes in pitch position, drums as percussion marks.

Melody replay

Pause the physics and play back exactly what was transcribed, note by note, with each one highlighted on the staff as it sounds.

Binary MIDI export

Download your transcription as a standard .mid file, built byte by byte in the browser, and open it in any DAW or notation app.

  • 125 BPM
  • 480 PPQ
  • GM steel guitar
  • drums on ch. 10

Real-time synthesis

No samples are loaded. Guitar tones use detuned oscillators with a sub layer and filter envelope, drums come from shaped noise and pitch sweeps, and a master compressor holds the mix together.

One file, zero setup

The whole instrument is a single HTML file using Canvas and the Web Audio API. Open it locally or host it anywhere static.

Spotlight

Space FX, under the hood

The ✦ Space FX toggle in the top HUD opens a wet send bus. Guitar plucks and percussion feed a cross-feedback stereo delay, and both the delay and the direct send flow into a procedurally generated stereo reverb.

  • 270 msLeft delay tap
  • 405 msRight delay tap, a dotted ratio of the left for ping-pong motion
  • ~2.4 kHzLow-pass damping inside the feedback loop keeps repeats warm
  • 2.8 sStereo impulse response with early reflections and an exponentially decaying noise tail

How it works

From first click to a MIDI file

No tutorial needed, but here's the loop.

Drop orbs

Click any empty space to drop an orb at your cursor, use + Drop Orb, or let Auto-Drop rain them in.

Shape the path

Click a string or drum lane to place a deflector. Click it again to rotate it, and tune the fall with the Gravity slider.

Pick a mood

Switch between Pentatonic, Dorian and Major, then flip on ✦ Space FX for delay and reverb.

Keep the take

Watch the staff fill in, press ▶ Replay Melody to hear it back, and ⬇ Export MIDI to keep it.
